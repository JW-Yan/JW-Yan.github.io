<br/><br/><br/> We are a highly interdisciplinary team led by Dr. Jingwen Yan, Professor and Director of Bioinformatics program at Indiana University Indianapolis (IUI). Our research focuses on integration of brain imaging and molecular -omics data for improved <ins>mechanism understanding </ins> and <ins>early detection</ins> of complex brain disorders like Alzheimer's. Our technical arm aims to develop novel computational models (when needed) for integration of -omics data with missing values, imbalanced distributions and heterogeneous types.
